Movie Poster Why I Love It / Why It's Iconic
Mamma Mia! Genuinely one of my favorite movies, so nostalgic. Witty, comforting and the music by ABBA is just top tier.
13 Going on 30 We all know this one is iconic. The music, the outfits, everything was perfect.
A Cinderella Story Chad Michael Murray running up the bleachers in the rain was every 2000s girl's dream.
She's the Man This movie is so funny. A little strange concept but ends up so cute in the end.
The Princess Diaries So cute, I loved this one as a kid. Anne Hathaway's makeover is definition of iconic, and I quote this movie with my sister all the time.
10 Things I Hate About You The scene of Heath Ledger singing on the bleachers says enough about this one. And I know Julia Stiles' poem by heart.
